# Study Consent Text

> Version: **1.0** — 2026-11-24. Owner: study lead.
> This is the exact text shown to participants. Changing it requires a new
> version, an ethics amendment if the study is running, and a note in
> [`STUDY_PROTOCOL.md`](STUDY_PROTOCOL.md) §5.
>
> The consent record stores the version and the timestamp of each choice. A
> participant can see what they agreed to at any time.

## Introduction shown before any question

We are asking you to take part in a research study about whether CurbCut is
useful over time. Taking part is your choice. You can say no, and you can stop
at any time, for any reason, without explaining and without losing anything
you were owed.

The study lasts twelve weeks. It involves five waves of questions and three
short tasks at three of those waves. Each wave takes about 20 minutes. You are
paid for each wave you complete, even if you later leave the study.

We do not record what is on your screen. We do not record your typing. We
record answers you give us, task results, and — only if you agree separately —
counts of how often you use the software.

## Layered choices

Each choice is separate. Refusing one does not refuse the others unless you
say so.

### 1. Study participation

We run the five waves and keep your questionnaire answers.

- **Yes, I agree to take part.**
- **No, I do not want to take part.**

If yes, we still ask again at week 4 and week 12. You can change your mind at
either point.

### 2. Clinician or carer informant

With your permission, we ask a clinician or carer who knows you to fill in a
short questionnaire about how you manage tasks. They do not see your answers.

- **Yes, you may contact my clinician/carer.**
- **No, do not contact them.**

You can change this independently of whether you stay in the study.

### 3. Usage counters

CurbCut already collects counts-only telemetry when a user opts in. For the
study, we would collect the same counts so we can see whether people keep using
the software. The counts carry no screen content and no text.

- **Yes, collect usage counts.**
- **No, do not collect them.**

Refusing does not change the software and does not end your participation.

### 4. Future contact

We may want to ask you about later studies.

- **Yes, you may contact me about later studies.**
- **No, do not contact me again.**

This does not affect anything else.

### 5. Keeping data after withdrawal

If you leave the study, we ask what should happen to answers we already have.

- **Delete everything I have given.**
- **Keep it as anonymous totals that cannot be traced to me.**

If you choose deletion, your answers are removed and the anonymous totals
already published are not changed. If you choose to keep it as anonymous
totals, no one can connect those totals to you.

## What happens if you stop

Tell us at any wave, by email, or through the moderator. We stop collecting
immediately. You keep payment for waves you finished. You can ask for your
answers to be deleted, using the choice above.

## Contact

Study lead: **study@curbcut.ai**. Ethics committee contact is shown on the
information sheet you received. If you are unhappy with the study, you can
contact the ethics committee directly.

## Agreement record

At enrollment we show a final screen:

- Study participation: yes / no
- Clinician/carer informant: yes / no
- Usage counts: yes / no
- Future contact: yes / no
- On withdrawal: delete / keep anonymous totals

and we store the version (1.0), the timestamp, and each answer. We give you a
copy.
